# Under the Needle — motion study 01

A separate, noindex `/intro-preview/` route previews a nine-second camera flight over the existing turntable. The homepage intro remains unchanged. The sequence starts at the gold studs, passes under the moving cartridge, then pulls back to the established resting camera. It is silent and starts on request; replay, pause and scrubbing are available. Reduced-motion users see the final shot first.

The preview alone adds 448 rounded gold stud faces and matching recesses as instanced geometry attached to the rotating platter, plus filtered procedural vinyl grooves. Existing animation tracks and materials remain shared with the current scene preparation. Resolution uses the existing bounded Retina render policy. The renderer pauses when hidden and releases resources on unmount.

This is a camera study for owner review, not approval to replace the homepage sequence. Physical iPhone Safari performance and final music/website transition remain to be reviewed before promotion. Desktop browser checks cover 320, 390, 1050 and 1440 widths; camera tests check continuity, low-pass clearance, deterministic scrubbing, needle/spin timing and final camera placement.
