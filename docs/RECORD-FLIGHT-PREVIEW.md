# Under the Needle — motion study 02

A separate, noindex `/intro-preview/` route previews a nine-second camera flight over the existing turntable. The homepage intro remains unchanged. The sequence starts at groove level, with one straight outward camera glide as the cartridge passes overhead. The needle lands at four seconds and the record then turns during the remaining pullback to the established resting camera. Camera position and aim interpolate continuously without an orbit or reversing spline. It is silent and starts on request; replay, pause and scrubbing are available. Reduced-motion users see the final shot first.

The preview alone adds 448 rounded gold stud faces and matching recesses as instanced geometry attached to the rotating platter, plus independently filtered coarse and fine procedural vinyl grooves that remain visible in the wide shot. Existing animation tracks and materials remain shared with the current scene preparation. Resolution uses the existing bounded Retina render policy. The renderer pauses when hidden and releases resources on unmount.

This is a camera study for owner review, not approval to replace the homepage sequence. Physical iPhone Safari performance and final music/website transition remain to be reviewed before promotion. Desktop browser checks cover 320, 390, 1050 and 1440 widths; camera tests check continuity, low-pass clearance, deterministic scrubbing, needle/spin timing and final camera placement.
