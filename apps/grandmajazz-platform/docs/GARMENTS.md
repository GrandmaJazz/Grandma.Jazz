## Implementation

- React/Wouter route in the existing Express/Vite application; Three.js is lazy loaded only for the 3D view. Existing homepage receives one Garments link.
- A single magazine group moves from its shelf position to the reader. Opening uses the same deformable mesh as later turns. Closing rotates the current turned stack, exposing the actual outside cover before returning to the saved shelf pose.
- CPU-deformed 96 by 24 segment paper meshes; normals and WebGL shadow/depth passes use the actual deformed positions. The reverse face has distinct artwork and flipped U coordinates, not a mirrored front image.
- Cylindrical travelling bend integrates the paper tangent, retaining horizontal fibre lengths and anchoring every row at the spine. Corner position modulates bend radius. This is an original controlled geometric implementation, not Google's implementation or a physics simulation.
- Mouse/touch/pen pointer capture, cancellation/reversal/flick decisions, keyboard and button turns share one engine. Zoom and pinch/pan arbitrate separately. Camera settles after each transition; there is no idle rendering loop.
- Texture loading buffers six adjacent page faces, evicts other full-size pages, and keeps lightweight shelf covers. Asset errors have retry and accessible-reader paths. WebGL failure/context loss uses the accessible reader.
- Low measured animation cadence reduces shadow resolution and pixel ratio before reducing paper geometry quality.

## Page Ordering

Logical faces: front cover, ordered interior assets, back cover. If needed an explicit blank is inserted BEFORE the back cover. Sheet s has front 2s and reverse 2s+1.
At k turned sheets, the visible spread is left 2k-1 and right 2k. k=0 is the closed front cover; k=N is the closed back cover.
Narrow layouts focus one logical face. Moving from a left reverse face to the already-visible right front face pans across the same spread; advancing from that right face curls the next physical sheet. No reverse faces are skipped. Resizing preserves the current logical face.
